Hello,

This is my first fanfic.

Some time ago I had an idea for a Harry Potter AU novel (working title *Ouroboros*). I shelved it, partly because I'm busy writing my own novels. However the recent wave of female Draco ideas awakened something in me. First, because that one change, makes my old idea so much better. Second, because I believe it would have changed the books in fascinating ways. So many things just start to fit so well.

It isn't mainly about the romance. As a teenage boy I was annoyed by romance scenes myself. When you think about it, it actually hinders the romance as it would be chained up for seven books and released only at the very last moment. What sells it to me is how much more sense the plot and the relationships make. Two things above all:

1. **It mirrors Snape and Lily.** History doesn't repeat itself, but it rhymes: an impossible Gryffindor–Slytherin bond, the other way around. Think how this would change the character dynamic of Snape-Potter-Malfoy and at one point Dumbledore as well.
2. **The Malfoy redemption arc.**

And the redemption arc I now present to you. *Book 7, chapter 36, the Battle of Hogwarts: Voldemort has announced that Harry Potter is dead, and Draconia believes him.*
